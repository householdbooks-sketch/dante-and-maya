How to Play on Your Devices

Select your device below for simple, clear instructions on downloading and listening.

1

Option A: Tap & Listen Instantly (Fastest)

Scroll up to the In-Browser Master Player on this page. Tap play on any track—it plays directly in Safari/Chrome in full stereo quality with your screen locked or in the background.

2

Option B: Download to iPhone Files App

Tap the Download MP3 button next to any song. When Safari asks "Do you want to download...?", tap Download.

3

Find & Play in Files

Open the blue Files app on your iPhone -> Go to Downloads. Tap any song to play instantly! You can also save or share it directly from Files.

Pro Tip for Apple Music on iPhone: Apple restricts iOS Safari from automatically injecting downloaded MP3s directly into the Apple Music app. To add these songs to your official Apple Music library on iPhone, open your computer (Mac or PC), download the files, and drag them into the Apple Music app (or iTunes). They will automatically sync to your iPhone!
1

Download MP3s or Full ZIP

Tap Download All (.ZIP Bundle) or tap Download MP3 next to individual songs. Chrome will download the files directly to your device.

2

Open in Your Music App

Open My Files or Files by Google -> Go to Downloads. Tap any MP3 or extract the ZIP. The songs will automatically appear in your preferred music player (YouTube Music, VLC, Samsung Music, or Poweramp).

1

Download the Complete ZIP Bundle

Click Download All (.ZIP Bundle) at the top of this page. Your browser will download Speak_Life_EP_Digital_Master_Package.zip.

2

Extract the Folder

On Mac: Double-click the ZIP file to extract.
On Windows: Right-click the ZIP file and select "Extract All...".

3

Drag into Your Music Library

Drag the extracted MP3 files directly into Apple Music (Mac) or Windows Media Player / Spotify Local Files (Windows). They will remain permanently in your local library.
