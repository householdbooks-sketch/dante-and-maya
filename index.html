The Movement

Who Are Dante & Maya?

Two visionaries uniting trade craftsmanship, agriculture, and faith to build true community food sovereignty.

Dante Hollis

The Master Builder • Journeyman Carpenter

A skilled carpenter and tradesman who knows the physical weight of construction. Confronted by institutional disinvestment, Dante refuses to let his neighborhood remain dependent on corner bodegas. Armed with his tools and covenant faith, he chooses to build the infrastructure himself.

Maya Owens

The Visionary Strategist • Community Advocate

The driving force behind Delaware Harvest. Maya bridges local agriculture, cooperative economics, and media advocacy. She ensures that community resources circulate locally and that food security is recognized as a fundamental pillar of spiritual and economic health.

Delaware Harvest Co-Op

Food Sovereignty • Economic Independence

A cooperative grocery initiative built from the ground up to replace commercial food deserts with fresh produce, locally sourced nutrition, and living-wage jobs. Funded by faith, labor, and community trust rather than exploitative institutional debt.
