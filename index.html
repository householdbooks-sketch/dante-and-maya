In My Hand (Everything We Need)

Dante & Maya • Track 03 Lead Single • Grounded in 2 Peter 1:3 & Exodus 14:16

Verse 1

I was looking for a breakthrough that was miles away
Staring at a mountain, begging it to move today
I told the Lord I'm empty, I don't have the tool
Standing in my weakness, feeling like a fool
But He whispered in the silence, "Look at what you hold"
An ordinary vessel can carry purest gold.

Chorus

'Cause You've given us everything we need
For life and godliness, the shadow has to flee
When fear starts talking, we'll remember the wins
Where You brought us from to where we are again
We don't have to wonder if Your grace will stand
The power's in Your promise, and the tool is in my hand.

Verse 2

Just a simple wooden staff parted up the sea
Just five loaves of bread fed the hungry company
You take the weak and foolish things to confound the wise
You don't need a giant's sword to make the morning rise
So I'm reaching for the memories of how You pulled me through
Every single victory points me back to You.

Bridge

Anxiety may whisper that the supply is running low
But I can count the Ebenezer stones along the road
From where I was to where I am, Your track record is true
I'm trading all my heavy fear for daily trust in You.

Learning the trade under Marcus Vance, who convinced him to turn his life around for good and taught him PowerNomics principles. Convinced to invest his money back into the community, Dante was captivated by Maya's vision to acquire property and build a grocery store from the ground up.
